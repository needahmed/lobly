LEGAL / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Data we store

  • Email address and password hash for LOBLY login
  • Riot PUUID, Riot ID, summoner ID, and platform
  • Match-derived stats (rank, roles, champions, form)
  • Listings, join requests, and session membership
  • Session chat (retained up to 7 days)
  • Endorsements, reliability events, blocks, and reports

What we do not store

We do not collect real legal names. Discord tags are optional and only shared according to your reveal preference. League Client (LCU) port and authentication token never leave your machine.

Purpose

Data is used to verify profiles, run filters and Match Score, automate lobby invites you approve, operate chat and reputation features, and improve the reliability of the network.

Deletion

You can delete your account via the app (DELETE /v1/users/me) or by emailing support@lobly.gg. Deletion removes linked Riot accounts, snapshots, listings, chat, and blocks; report rows may be retained with the reporter field cleared for moderation integrity.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to privacy@lobly.gg.
